## Current user priority (20 October 2026)

### M1 — Minimum usable multiphysics engine

**User decision, 22 October 2026:** establish a finite minimum and move into Transform/Solve as soon as its deduction prerequisites are met. This milestone supersedes earlier instructions to extend coefficient-case partitions or complete every deduction adapter before proceeding. **Status, 23 October 2026: Claude approved all five D1 criteria for frozen M1-A/B/C and independently verified U1 for the same fixed suite; M1 is complete for M1-A/B/C.** Existing scoped capabilities remain available, but their passing tests do not establish this complete acceptance suite.

**Objective:** a user can build, replay, transform, solve and inspect a small coupled nonspatial model in the existing worksheet, from fundamental laws and explicit constitutive premises, using one versioned formulation with verified units, validity conditions and invalidation. M1 covers finite-dimensional first-order explicit ODE systems with algebraic quantities that can be uniquely reconstructed in a declared regular regime. Existing supported thermodynamic closures remain compatible; M1 does not require developing a new equilibrium package or general DAE solver.

There are two separate gates. **D1 ends general deduction polishing and starts Transform/Solve implementation. U1 establishes usable end-to-end multiphysics.** A missing compiler or solver bridge is a U1 task and cannot, by itself, justify extending the symbolic kernel after D1.

#### D1 — Deduction readiness and stop rule

D1 passes only when **all three** acceptance documents satisfy these criteria:

1. Every active balance starts at its relevant fundamental law. Each used physical assumption and mathematical step has replayable inputs and actual before/after expressions; definitions, constitutive premises and conditions retain their distinct roles. Audit chronology for the controls used by these documents. Completing every other physical adapter is not required.
2. Units are known and consistent. Needed substitutions, sums, product/chain operations, integration/reduction and unique isolation are supported through shared tools. Denominators, branch choices and domain assumptions are explicit; unsupported regimes are rejected. No exhaustive parameter partition is needed when a declared, checkable regular regime suffices.
3. The selected system identifies states, their first derivatives, reconstructible quantities, parameters, initial/boundary requirements and closure dependencies with stable IDs. It has no unexplained source terms or unresolved constitutive obligations in its selected regime. Redundant totals and dependent consequences are marked. Missing numerical values are reported for Solve, not invented by Model.
4. Replay, removal/change of a used assumption, Undo/Redo, persistence and same-ID source changes behave correctly. The exact selected graph and conditions reach the Transform boundary. An invalid history remains inspectable and cannot execute a valid prefix or older system.
5. Independent algebra/reference checks pass and no confirmed soundness defect remains on the admitted paths. Claude's audit supplies evidence and counterexamples; it is not an unlimited feature request. Record any unresolved concern that prevents these specific claims as a located blocker.
